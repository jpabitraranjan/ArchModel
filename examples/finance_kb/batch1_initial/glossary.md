# Trading & Fund Glossary

- **Authorised Participant**: A licensed market maker or institution permitted to create and redeem ETF units directly with the fund manager (also: AP, authorized partner).
- **Net Flow**: Purchases minus sales of a security by a given participant group over a period; positive means net buying (also: net buying, net position change).
- **Creation**: Delivery of a basket of securities by an Authorised Participant in exchange for new ETF units.
- **Redemption**: Return of ETF units by an Authorised Participant in exchange for the underlying basket of securities.
- **Trade Date**: The date an order is executed, as opposed to the settlement date when ownership transfers.
- **Settlement Date**: The date a trade is finalised and securities and cash change hands, normally T+1 or T+2.
- **GICS Sector**: Global Industry Classification Standard sector assigned to a security (for example Financials, Energy, Information Technology).
- **Portfolio Manager**: Employee accountable for the holdings and performance of one or more portfolios; the primary user of trading analytics.
- **Notional Value**: Quantity multiplied by price at execution, expressed in the trade currency.
