# Data Access and Retention Policy

## Access control
Trading data may be viewed only by staff holding the Portfolio Manager, Trader or Compliance role. Participant-level (AP) trading data is classified Restricted and requires an additional entitlement approved by Compliance.

## Retention
Trade records must be retained for 7 years and be available for regulatory inspection.

## Audit
Access to Restricted data must be logged with user, timestamp and query, and audit logs retained for 7 years.

## Data residency
Restricted data must not leave the approved regional data centre.
